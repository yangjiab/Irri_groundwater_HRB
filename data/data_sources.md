# Data Sources

## Atmospheric Data

Atmospheric forcing data used in this study were obtained from the National Cryosphere Desert Data Center of China:

http://www.ncdc.ac.cn

An independent evapotranspiration data is used for Aquacrop modeling perfromance evaluation:

Zhang, C., Long, D., Zhang, Y., Anderson, M.C., Kustas, W.P., Yang, Y., 2021. A decadal (2008–2017) daily evapotranspiration data set of 1 km spatial resolution and spatial completeness across the North China Plain using TSEB and data fusion. Remote Sensing of Environment 262, 112519. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rse.2021.112519

## Cropland Data

Cropland distribution datasets were obtained from the National Earth System Science Data Center of China:

http://www.nesdc.org.cn

## Soil hydraulic conductivity data

Soil hydraulic conductivity data used in this study is from the global high-resolution soil hydraulic and thermal property dataset:

Dai, Y., Xin, Q., Wei, N., Zhang, Y., Shangguan, W., Yuan, H., Zhang, S., Liu, S., Lu, X., 2019. A Global High-Resolution Data Set of Soil Hydraulic and Thermal Properties for Land Surface Modeling. Journal of Advances in Modeling Earth Systems 11, 2996–3023. https://doi.org/10.1029/2019MS001784

## Agricultural Record Data

Agricultural statistical records were collected from the Hebei Statistical Yearbook:

http://tjj.hebei.gov.cn/hbstjj/sj/sjcx/tjnj/

## Soil type data

The soil property data used for AquaCrop simulations were derived from the Harmonized World Soil Database version 2.0 (HWSD v2.0):

FAO and IIASA, 2023. Harmonized World Soil Database version 2.0. Rome, Italy and Laxenburg, Austria. https://doi.org/10.4060/cc3823en

## Groundwater Observation Data

Groundwater observation data are subject to policy restrictions and cannot be publicly shared in their original form.

## Other Data

The administrative boundary map of China used in this study was approved under the cartographic review number:

GS(2019)1822
